Johnston

Johnston, Rhode Island, is a suburban town just southwest of Providence, offering a blend of natural beauty, rich history, and modern amenities. The town is home to several parks, including Johnston War Memorial Park, which features walking paths, sports fields, and a pond, and Snake Den State Park, a 1,000-acre area with hiking trails, a working farm, and a wild tulip farm. For those interested in local history, the Clemence–Irons House, built in 1691, is a rare example of colonial architecture and is listed on the National Register of Historic Places. Johnston also offers a variety of recreational activities, including seasonal events at Pezza Farm, which has been operating since 1947 and offers hayrides, pumpkins, and fresh produce. With its proximity to Providence, Johnston provides a peaceful suburban lifestyle with easy access to urban amenities
