Newport

Newport, Rhode Island, is a picturesque coastal city known for its rich history, stunning scenery, and elegant charm—making it a top vacation destination. Visitors can explore the city's famous Gilded Age mansions like The Breakers and Marble House, which offer a glimpse into America's opulent past. The scenic Cliff Walk, a 3.5-mile trail along the rocky shoreline, combines ocean views with historic architecture for a truly memorable experience. Newport also boasts a thriving food scene, including fresh seafood at waterfront restaurants and historic spots like the White Horse Tavern, one of the oldest in the country. Outdoor lovers can visit Fort Adams State Park for panoramic bay views or enjoy wine tastings at Newport Vineyards. With its mix of cultural attractions, natural beauty, and seaside charm, Newport offers something special for every type of traveler.
