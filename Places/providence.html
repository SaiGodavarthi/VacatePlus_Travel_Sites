Providence

Providence, Rhode Island, is a charming coastal city known for its rich history, vibrant arts scene, and delicious culinary offerings. Explore beautifully preserved colonial architecture, stroll along the scenic Riverwalk, and experience the magic of WaterFire (an award-winning fire sculpture installation on the rivers downtown). With its blend of culture, creativity, and New England charm, Providence is the perfect getaway for people looking for good food, enthralling history, and beautiful art.
